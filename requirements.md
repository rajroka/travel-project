Smart Tourism Management System for a Single Tour Company
Project Overview
The Smart Tourism Management System is a web-based application developed for a single tour company to digitize and simplify tourism operations. The system allows customers to explore destinations, browse tour packages, book tours, receive AI-generated travel itineraries, and manage their bookings online.
The company owner and staff manage destinations, packages, bookings, customers, website content, and business analytics through a secure administrative dashboard.
The AI Trip Planner recommends only the tour packages already offered by the company, ensuring customers always book existing company packages.

AI Trip Planner
The AI Trip Planner helps customers plan trips using the company's existing tour packages.
Customer Inputs
•	Destination 
•	Budget 
•	Number of Days 
•	Interests 
Example
Destination: Pokhara
Days: 4
Budget: $350
Interest: Adventure

AI Generates
•	Matching company packages 
•	Day-by-day itinerary 
•	Recommended attractions 
•	Suggested restaurants 
•	Estimated daily expenses 
•	Packing checklist 
•	Travel tips 
Note: The AI only recommends packages that already exist in the company's database. Customers can only book available company tour packages.

11. Payment Module
The Payment Module allows customers to securely pay for tour bookings through multiple payment methods. It also enables staff and the company owner to monitor payment transactions and verify payment status.
Customer
Customers can:
•	Select payment method 
•	Pay online during booking 
•	Pay later (optional, depending on company policy) 
•	View payment status 
•	Download payment receipt/invoice 
•	View payment history 
•	Receive payment confirmation email 
Supported Payment Methods
•	eSewa 
•	Khalti 
•	Stripe (International Cards) 
•	PayPal (Optional) 
•	Cash Payment (Offline) 

Staff
Staff can:
•	View payment transactions 
•	Verify offline payments 
•	Update payment status 
•	Generate invoices 
•	Issue refunds (if approved) 

Company Owner (Admin)
Admin can:
•	View all payment transactions 
•	Monitor total revenue 
•	View monthly payment reports 
•	Track pending payments 
•	Track completed payments 
•	View refunded payments 
•	Generate financial reports 
•	Export payment reports
